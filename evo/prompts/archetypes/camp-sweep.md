**Camp sweep.** Win by out-farming: clear camp groups in an efficient loop and bank gems often.
- Plan the loop at draft time: order groups by reward per unit of clear time (cheap groups with gem
  clusters first; patch 100 made plain wolves and elks worth few gems), and by travel distance.
- Draft champions that clear groups quickly (area damage and splash on grouped monsters, strong basic attacks).
- Tribute in small batches: 1–10 gems take one countdown tick, so banking often costs little and
  protects gems from being stolen on death.
- Respawn timers (40–150 rounds) mean a group is empty for a while after capture: route to the next group,
  do not wait.
- Risk: an aggressive opponent can catch spread-out farmers; keep an eye on enemies in vision and recall when low.
