**Vision and ambush.** Win by seeing more than the opponent and picking off isolated champions.
- Every champion has its own vision (8 tiles, shadowcasting; walls and bushes block it) plus one ward
  (10-round cooldown after patch 100). Vision is not shared, so use the 8-byte shared memory to pass
  sightings between your champions.
- Wait in bushes near altars or camps the enemy uses, and attack champions who arrive carrying gems.
- Ward the approaches to your own farming area.
- Risk: waiting costs farming time. Ambush only where the enemy actually goes, and farm otherwise.
