**Map reader.** Win by adapting the whole plan to each map at draft time.
- The draft sees the full map with 10x fuel. Measure map size, camp composition (clusters, golems,
  monolith), distances from spawn to camps and altars, and the number of champions per team.
- Choose the draft and the plan from those numbers: big-camp hunting when golems or a monolith are near,
  farming loops on cluster-rich maps, aggression on small maps.
- Write the plan into the 1024-byte draft memory so every champion follows the same plan.
- Risk: complex plans with weak execution. Keep each plan simple and test on very different maps.
