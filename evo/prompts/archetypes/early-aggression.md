**Early aggression.** Win by killing enemy champions: each kill pays 100 gold plus all gems the victim
carries (patch 100), and a dead champion sits out 60 rounds.
- Draft champions with early power: high base stats, stuns, dashes, attack resets.
- Move as a group towards the enemy's likely first camps, and strike when they are hurt from fighting monsters.
- Melee champions can move and attack in the same turn; ranged champions must choose. Use that in chases.
- Keep farming between fights so you do not fall behind on gold, and convert kill gold into shards.
- Risk: losing an even fight hands the enemy the same rewards. Disengage from fights you are losing.
