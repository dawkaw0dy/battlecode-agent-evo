**Altar control.** Win by protecting your tributes and denying theirs.
- Altar locks (10 gold) block the opponent's tributes at that altar, cancel their running tribute
  channels, and take 5 basic attacks to clear (patch 100). Your own team can still tribute there.
- Keep one champion near the altars the enemy uses, lock them when enemies approach carrying gems,
  and punish the champions who stop to clear the lock.
- Farm with the others and tribute at altars you control.
- Damage does not stop a tribute, but a stun, death or a new lock does.
- Risk: spending too many champions on denial. Measure whether a lock actually cost the opponent gems.
