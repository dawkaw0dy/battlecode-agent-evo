**Scaling economy.** Win late: gather gold fast, turn it into stats, and dominate after the midgame.
- Vesper Vale gains 33% more gold (patch 100), and a Treasure emblem adds 20 percentage points more.
- Gold clusters and wolves carry gold. Lifetime gold earned is also the first tiebreak at round 2000.
- Buy shards as soon as you are on spawn or dead; five matching shards make an emblem.
- Choose the stat that matches each champion's growth values (GameRules.md, as changed by the patches).
- Risk: falling too far behind on gems early. Keep tributing; the 1000-gem win ends the game early.
