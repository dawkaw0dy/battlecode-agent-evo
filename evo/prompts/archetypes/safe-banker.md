**Safe banker.** Win by never losing gems: carried gems are lost when a camp kills you and stolen when an
enemy does; tributed gems are permanent.
- Tribute as soon as you carry gems and an altar is close; 1–10 gems take a single countdown tick.
- Recall early (5 turn ends; 3 with the Home emblem), and heal on spawn.
- Avoid fights unless you clearly win them; retreat when enemies come into vision.
- Buy Health and Durability (10% less incoming damage) to survive mistakes.
- Risk: being too passive against an opponent that farms faster; keep the farming loop busy.
