Camp farming: which groups to clear first, in what order, and with how many champions. Weigh health,
damage and range against the reward (gems, gold, respawn time) using the camp tables in GameRules.md as
changed by patch 100 (wolf, elk, gem and gold clusters, golem, monolith). Remember the capture rules: stand on a
spawn tile uncontested for five capture phases, rewards split among allies present, the whole group respawns together.
