### Archetype: deathball
**Deathball.** Win by moving and fighting together: the whole team travels as one group, clears each
camp group fast and wins every fight through numbers.
- Pick a lead champion; the others follow it and stay within a few tiles.
- Combine stuns, area damage, shields and heals; choose a draft whose kits support each other.
- Rewards split among allies present at capture, so the gold and gems per champion stay even.
- Take fights only together, and chase down isolated enemies.
- Risk: slow coverage of large maps; the group must keep moving between nearby groups.
