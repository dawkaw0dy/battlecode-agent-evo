### Archetype: split-farm
**Split farm.** Win by covering the map: each champion farms its own region on its own.
- At draft time, split the camp groups into one region per champion by distance from spawn and altars,
  and write the assignment into draft memory.
- Draft self-sufficient champions that can clear groups alone (sustain, strong basic attacks).
- Each champion tributes at the altar nearest to its region.
- Regroup only when the shared memory reports an enemy invading a region.
- Risk: a grouped enemy team beats lone champions. Retreat and recall rather than fight alone.
