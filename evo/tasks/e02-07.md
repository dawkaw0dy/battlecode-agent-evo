### Archetype: big-game-hunter
**Big-game hunter.** Win by taking golems and the monolith, the camps with by far the largest rewards
(golem 36 gems and 160 gold; monolith 200 gems and 300 gold, after patch 100).
- Draft burst damage and sustain: Focus-scaling spells, shields, heals; consider Jungle (30% less damage
  from camps) and Monster (10% more damage to camps) emblems.
- Farm gold early on cheap groups, buy shards, then commit everyone to the big camp together.
- Mind the ranges: golem 4, monolith 5, so ranged champions cannot hit them safely from outside their range.
- A camp kill destroys your carried gems: tribute before a risky fight.
- Risk: maps without these camps; fall back to normal farming when there is none.
