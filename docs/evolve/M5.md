## What this shows

- **Selection is now clearly working:** the best child e02-01 rates above its parent e01-01 with **non-overlapping**
  intervals ([1.03, 1.55] vs [−0.22, 0.06]), and five of six children beat both hall-of-fame bots.
- **No sign of tuning to the visible maps:** the top four win as often or more often on the 16 held-out maps they never saw.
- The two duplicate briefs gave different results (e02-01 rank 1 vs e02-04 rank 8; e02-02 rank 3 vs e02-03 rank 5):
  the same brief still explores different solutions.
- **Cost:** 7 sessions; Actions about 2 + 5 minutes. **The local spot-check was the slowest part**: 33 Stage B games took
  about 48 minutes on the PC (cells are replayed one after another). A follow-up could run cells in parallel.
- Stage C is implemented and unit-tested, but has not run live yet (its condition was not met).
