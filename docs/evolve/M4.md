### HALT on g01-B (21:12 UTC) — an orchestrator bug, not bad results

`g01-B` (`5df4587`, 12 shards, 576 requested games) failed verification with `duplicate game ('e01-01', 'e00-01', …)`.
Cause: the elite e00-01 is both in the Stage B top (round robin) and in the panel (hall of fame), and
`stage_b_pairs` planned its games twice: 48 games requested twice. The panel part also replayed e00-01 vs
e01-02/e01-03 in reverse orientation. Every duplicate pair was **identical**, replay bytes included, and no shard was missing.
The halt worked as designed: it stopped on an unexpected result before anything was cached or rated.

Fix `dca675e`: stage plans skip panel bots that are in the top and dedupe unordered (pair, map) cells;
verification accepts identical repeats of games a job requested twice and flags repeats that differ;
`python -m evo unhalt --reason …` clears a halt. With the fix, `python -m evo verify g01-B` reports no problems.
The owner agreed; `unhalt` after the fix, and the next step re-verified g01-B (no problems), spot-checked 12/12 matching, rated and selected.
